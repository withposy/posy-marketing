## Why couples go looking

GuestPix does the core job well, and unlike most of this category it does not
count uploads on any package. If you are here, it is usually one of two numbers.

**The three month window on Classic.** Classic is $49 and it is the tier most
people land on when sorting by price. Uploads close after three months. That is
usually fine, and occasionally it is not, because the photos that arrive latest
tend to be the ones nobody else took.

**Flat pricing.** Packages are priced per gallery, so guest count never moves
the figure. A large wedding gets a bargain. A thirty-person elopement pays the
same $49.

## What to check on any alternative

**Does the cheapest plan cap uploads?** GuestPix does not, and that is a real
strength. Several cheaper-looking rivals do.

**Two clocks, not one.** How long guests can upload, and how long you can
download afterwards. They are frequently different numbers.

**Does the price move with your guest list?** Flat pricing favours large
weddings. Per-guest pricing favours small ones. Neither is better in the
abstract, only for your day.
