## Why couples go looking

Wedibox is a one-time payment, it has a real free tier, and it embeds into a
site you already own. Those are genuine advantages. The reason people still end
up comparing is the upload window.

**It moves with the plan.** Three months on Embed at $29, six months on Wedding
at $49, twelve months on All-In-One at $79. If you compared entry prices and
assumed the clock was the same across the range, it is worth a second look
before you buy.

**The free tier is a trial, not a wedding.** Fifty guest photo uploads is enough
to prove the idea works. A hundred guests sharing one photo each passes it twice
over.

## What to check on any alternative

**How long can guests upload?** This is the number that varies most across this
category, and it is rarely the headline.

**How long can you download afterwards?** A separate clock, often a different
figure.

**Does the cheapest plan cap uploads?** Some do, some do not. It is worth
knowing which before the day rather than after.
