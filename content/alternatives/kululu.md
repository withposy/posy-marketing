## Why couples go looking

Kululu works. Guests scan a code, a page opens, photos go up. If you are here,
it is usually not because the app failed. It is because of one of two numbers.

**The upload cap.** The free plan stops at a hundred photos and videos. Plus stops
at five hundred. Five hundred sounds generous, and then you do the arithmetic:
a hundred guests taking five photos each is exactly five hundred. Guests have no
idea a cap exists, so the people who hit it are whoever uploads last, which is
usually the ones still dancing.

**The clock.** On the free plan, uploads are kept for seven days and the event
stays open for twenty-four hours. Plus keeps three months, Pro keeps a year.
Wedding photos have a habit of surfacing late, when someone finally clears their
camera roll.

## What to actually compare

Ignore the headline price for a moment and check three things on any app you
look at, including ours.

**Does the cheapest plan cap uploads?** Plenty of apps advertise a low entry
price that quietly limits how much your guests can share.

**How long do you keep the files?** There are two clocks, not one: how long
guests can upload, and how long you can download afterwards. They are often
different numbers.

**Are downloads full resolution?** A gallery you cannot get the originals out of
is a rental, not an album.
