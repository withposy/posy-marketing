# Posy pricing

Posy is a wedding photo-sharing gallery. Guests scan one QR code and upload every photo and video to a single private gallery, with no app and no login. You keep the gallery for 12 months and can download everything in full resolution.

- Currency: USD
- Billing: one-time payment (not a subscription)
- Access: 12 months from sign-up
- Last updated: 2026-07-06

## Plans

The only difference between plans is the guest count. Every feature listed under "Included in every plan" is identical across all three.

| Plan | Price | Billing | Guests | Best for |
|------|-------|---------|--------|----------|
| Tiny Toasts | $29 | one-time, 12 months | up to 50 | intimate weddings, close family and dearest friends |
| Champagne Towers | $59 | one-time, 12 months | up to 150 | mid-sized weddings (most couples choose this) |
| Happily Ever After | $99 | one-time, 12 months | 200+ | larger weddings, every cousin and every plus-one |

## Included in every plan

- Unlimited photos and videos
- Real-time social feed with comments
- Best moments and most talked about, surfaced automatically
- No app to install, no guest registration
- Your own private QR code and link
- Full-resolution downloads for 12 months

## Billing

- One-time payment. No subscriptions, no rolling charges.
- Full access for 12 months from the day you sign up.
- Download everything in full resolution, anytime during those 12 months.

## Privacy

Your gallery is private to your QR code. No public feed and no algorithms, only the people you invite.

## FAQ

**Is this a subscription?**
No. You pay once and keep the gallery for 12 months.

**Which plan do I need?**
Pick by guest count: up to 50 is Tiny Toasts ($29), up to 150 is Champagne Towers ($59), 200 or more is Happily Ever After ($99).

**Do guests need an app or an account?**
No. Guests scan the QR code and upload from their phone browser, with no app and no login.

**Can I download the photos?**
Yes, in full resolution, anytime for 12 months.
